# Transcription Compiler and Robustness Harness Audit

Original audit: 21 November 2026. Integration corrections: 23 November 2026.

Scope: `transcription/scripts/batch_transcribe.py`, the EVA to ZFD compiler;
`transcription/scripts/generate_reports.py`; and
`validation/transcription_robustness/`. The current corpus has 196 automated
packages, five retained hand-anchor packages and two stubs: 203 packages in total.

Every number below was measured by running the code against the repo's own data.
Tags: **Verified** = reproduced by running code. **Inferred** = follows from the code
but not run on the target system.

The original audit recorded 4,795 of 4,965 rows matching the old compiler.
Those figures describe the pre-fix corpus, not the current edition. The impact
counts in C1–C15 and T1–T5 below are likewise the historical measurements from
that audit. Current reproduction and denominator checks are recorded in F.

## D. Canon decisions (resolved from the repo, 2026-11-21)

D1. **sh -> š, in every position.** Every canonical source spells it š:
`zfd_decoder/data/lexicon_v2.csv` (the declared root source: šor, šei, še, šeo, šeom),
`08_Final_Proofs/Master_Key/unified_lexicon_v3.json` (operator š, state markers še/šeo),
`zfd_decoder/data/operators.json` and `mid_word.json` (sh -> š as operator and mid-word),
`06_Pipelines/glagolitic_ocr/character_reference.py` (Sha /ʃ/ -> š),
`mapping/FINAL_CHARACTER_MAP_v1.md` (/š/, shol = š-ol), `hand_sheet/glyph_inventory.json` (Sha),
and `zfd_decoder_v2.py`. The "s" and "s/sh" in abbreviation_rules.json and
ABBREVIATION_INVENTORY.md were ASCII spellings of the same sound; the gold anchors
spell it "sh" in 63 of 64 cases. Both spec files are updated (rules v1.1.0).

D2. **ch -> h, in every position.** mid_word.json, abbreviation_rules ligature_units,
gold f88r (otorchety -> otrorhetri).

D3. **y -> i outside the suffix slot.** character_reference.py maps EVA y (Yer) to i
with no position condition, glyph_inventory.json gives /i/, DUCTUS_ANALYSIS 6.2 #3
says initial and final y are the same glyph, and the gold anchors map non-final y to i
in 31 of 31 cases. Croatian spelling has no y. Dissent, noted: `zfd_decoder_v2.py`
keeps y in its CRO layer, and lexicon_v2.csv has Latin-loan stems spelled with y
(syr, myr, hyss, glyc, amyl, dysent) plus ykal. That is a lexicon-matching layer,
not the transcription orthography, and the transcription compiler does no lexicon
lookup, so nothing here depends on it.

D4. **Corpus regenerated** under D1-D3: 196 automated folio packages (including the 6
multi-panel pages), FULL_CORPUS.jsonl (now built by generate_reports.py, in
manuscript order), STATISTICS.md, COMPARISON_REPORT.md, SECTION_COMPARISON.md.
After regeneration the automated folios contain 0 unconverted daiin and 0 raw y.
The remaining "sh" letter pairs come from EVA s + ch ("schey" -> "shei"), which is
s + h by the rules. `zfd_decoder_v2.py` maps EVA sch to "šh" instead; that is a
v2-only rule and is not adopted here.

D5. **Residual disagreement after the report correction: 114 of 735 words.**
The former 100-of-617 breakdown covered the incomplete comparison subset and
is superseded. Correct comma alignment adds 118 comparisons, of which 104 agree
and 14 disagree. The retained anchors have not been edited to improve the score.
A disagreement does not, by itself, establish whether the compiler or a hand
reading is correct; the current audit does not assign all 114 to one side.

## E. Shared-tokenizer follow-up

E1. **Closed in the 23 November integration.** The shared
`zfd_decoder/src/tokenizer.py` now removes raw-file metadata and inline markup,
respects drawing and comma boundaries, and removes `!` filler before generating
tokens. Its callers include `validation/blind_decode_test` and
`zfd_decoder/batch_decode.py`; the null-baseline generators use the same word
normalization. This is separate from the transcription compiler's EVA parser.

The integration rerun of the v3 blind-decode test retains the mean-score
hierarchy under both configurations, but the per-folio discrimination gate
passes **5/5 under the frozen configuration and 4/5 under the shipping
configuration**. The shipping f23v comparison against shuffled text has
z = 1.53. The former 5/5 shipping claim is superseded; see the updated
[dual-configuration report](../validation/blind_decode_test/results_v3/V3_DUAL_CONFIG_REPORT.md).

## F. Current reproduction and coverage (23 November 2026)

The 196 automated packages reproduce in all four generated files, allowing
only the generation date to change. The three aggregate reports and
`FULL_CORPUS.jsonl` also reproduce. The report corrections leave every retained
folio package and the concatenated corpus unchanged.

- **203 packages:** 196 automated, five hand anchors, two stubs.
- **5,194 JSONL rows:** includes one empty f85v diagram placeholder and one
  fragmentary f116v text-stub row. The latter is not diagram-only.
- **37,785 EVA tokens and 37,785 ZFD tokens** in the retained corpus.
- **8,125 unique EVA tokens and 7,844 unique ZFD tokens.**
- **621/735 anchor-token matches (84.5%)**, after the declared `sh` to `š`
  normalization, with all 129 anchor rows included.

The selector covers 201 page identifiers containing H transcription records,
but the protected anchors are not complete replacements for that selection.
In particular, f102v retains **38 rows and 180 EVA tokens**, whereas the current
source selector supplies **62 lines and 311 EVA tokens** for the page.
Across all selected source pages there are 5,215 source lines; the different
anchor representations and the two stub records explain why this is not the
same denominator as 5,194 retained JSONL rows. Page-identifier coverage must
not be presented as complete source-text coverage.

The f116v stub still contains its earlier heavy-uncertainty interpretation of
`!`, although the corrected compiler treats that character as filler. It is
retained legacy stub data, not regenerated compiler output. The hand anchors
and stubs require separate image-based review before their contents are changed.
