# Transcription Compiler and Robustness Harness Audit (2026-11-21)

Scope: `transcription/scripts/batch_transcribe.py` (the EVA to ZFD compiler that
produced 192 of the 197 folio packages), `transcription/scripts/generate_reports.py`,
and `validation/transcription_robustness/`.

Every number below was measured by running the code against the repo's own data.
Tags: **Verified** = reproduced by running code. **Inferred** = follows from the code
but not run on the target system.

The committed corpus is reproducible from the old compiler: 4,795 of 4,965 lines
match it exactly. The 170 that don't are the 5 hand anchors plus f4r, f5r and f5v,
which came from an older rule set (see C11).

## C. Robustness harness (validation/transcription_robustness)

| # | Bug | Status |
|---|-----|--------|
| T1 | Seeds use the built-in `hash()`, which Python randomizes per process, so results change between runs (for example f101v at 5%: 0.951 vs 0.960). | Verified, fixed (sha256 seeds) |
| T2 | Raw files go straight to the decoder. The tokenizer keeps every letter, so "=== Folio f10r ===" becomes tokens `folio` and `fr`, "[Text]" becomes `text`, and `<!plant>` is glued into words (`dyplantchy`). | T3 | The fingerprint can't fail the way the docstring claims. A coin-flip null decoder (stem = the raw word) earns LAYERED-REAL-SYSTEM. | T4 | The layer-stratified test compares decoder token i with EVA word i, but because of T2 they are 3 or more positions apart (up to 12 on f89r). Every measurement looked at the wrong word. | Verified, fixed (each word is decoded and compared against itself) |

## D. Canon decisions (resolved from the repo, 2026-11-21)

D1. **sh -> š, in every position.** Every canonical source spells it š:
`zfd_decoder/data/lexicon_v2.csv` (the declared root source: šor, šei, še, šeo, šeom),
`08_Final_Proofs/Master_Key/unified_lexicon_v3.json` (operator š, state markers še/šeo),
`zfd_decoder/data/operators.json` and `mid_word.json` (sh -> š as operator and mid-word),
`06_Pipelines/glagolitic_ocr/character_reference.py` (Sha /ʃ/ -> š),
`mapping/FINAL_CHARACTER_MAP_v1.md` (/š/, shol = š-ol), `hand_sheet/glyph_inventory.json` (Sha),
and `zfd_decoder_v2.py`. The "s" and "s/sh" in abbreviation_rules.json and
ABBREVIATION_INVENTORY.md were ASCII spellings of the same sound; the gold anchors
spell it "sh" in 63 of 64 cases. Both spec files are updated (rules v1.1.0).

D2. **ch -> h, in every position.** mid_word.json, abbreviation_rules ligature_units,
gold f88r (otorchety -> otrorhetri).

D3. **y -> i outside the suffix slot.** character_reference.py maps EVA y (Yer) to i
with no position condition, glyph_inventory.json gives /i/, DUCTUS_ANALYSIS 6.2 #3
says initial and final y are the same glyph, and the gold anchors map non-final y to i
in 31 of 31 cases. Croatian spelling has no y. Dissent, noted: `zfd_decoder_v2.py`
keeps y in its CRO layer, and lexicon_v2.csv has Latin-loan stems spelled with y
(syr, myr, hyss, glyc, amyl, dysent) plus ykal. That is a lexicon-matching layer,
not the transcription orthography, and the transcription compiler does no lexicon
lookup, so nothing here depends on it.

D4. **Corpus regenerated** under D1-D3: 196 folio packages (including the 6
multi-panel pages), FULL_CORPUS.jsonl (now built by generate_reports.py, in
manuscript order), STATISTICS.md, COMPARISON_REPORT.md, SECTION_COMPARISON.md.
After regeneration the automated folios contain 0 unconverted daiin and 0 raw y.
The remaining "sh" letter pairs come from EVA s + ch ("schey" -> "shei"), which is
s + h by the rules. `zfd_decoder_v2.py` maps EVA sch to "šh" instead; that is a
v2-only rule and is not adopted here.

D5. **Residual gold disagreement (100 of 617 words) is on the gold side:**
- 31: qo written as "koo" (qokor -> koostor), so the o is counted twice.
- 19: the anchors repeat the `!` filler error (cth!res -> htr[?]es).
- 13: other uncertainty notation ([special], [@252]).
- 37: other, mostly eey written "eei" against the spec's eey -> ei.
The anchors are hand work and are not edited here.

## E. Found, not fixed here (blast radius)

E1. `zfd_decoder/src/tokenizer.py` has the T2 behaviour for every caller that feeds it raw_eva files. That includes `validation/blind_decode_test` (v2/v3 runs) and `zfd_decoder/batch_decode.py`, so their coverage and known-ratio numbers include header and comment tokens. The fix is to strip header lines and `<...>` before tokenizing, then re-run those tests. Not checked: how much it moves their headline numbers.
